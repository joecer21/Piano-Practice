# Privacy

**The coach does not collect, send or sell anything about you.** There is no account, analytics, advertising, tracking pixel or server-side storage.

- **What stays on your device:** your last assignment, tempo, starred assignments, display and session preferences, and practice history (what you practised, when, for how long, at what tempo, and any notes you type). It is kept in this browser's local storage and never leaves the device unless you export it.
- **Share links** carry only the assignment's settings in the part of the address after `#`, which browsers do not send to the web server. Anyone you send a link to sees the assignment, nothing else.
- **MIDI:** the browser asks for permission only when you choose _Connect MIDI keyboard_. Notes are used to light the on-screen keys and, if you choose, to sound the app's piano. They are not recorded, stored or assessed.
- **Network requests:** the app itself, its local piano samples, and — only if you choose them in sound settings — the optional Fuhton or Salamander piano samples from `raw.githubusercontent.com` and `tonejs.github.io`. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, receives standard request information such as your IP address; see GitHub's privacy statement.
- **Removing your data:** _Recent practice → Clear history_ removes practice records. Clearing this site's data in your browser settings removes everything the coach stored.
